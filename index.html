More About Me

My Philosophy: Listen first, Code later

I don’t just « write code. » I help businesses breathe easier. My approach is simple: I listen to what’s blocking you, I understand the goal, and then I build the automation to fix it.

Whether it’s a high-scale Cloud system or a small business owner’s marketing, my goal is the same: I take care of the « heavy lifting » so you can focus on what you actually love doing.

Beyond the Code

I’m a creator at heart. When I’m not fine-tuning AI, I’m probably drawing or building something with my hands. There’s a special kind of satisfaction in seeing an idea become a real object (or a real line of code).

I also travel to keep my perspectives wide and watch a lot of cinema to understand different stories. I truly believe that to be a great engineer, you first have to be a curious and empathetic human being.

Let’s Build Something Meaningful Together!
